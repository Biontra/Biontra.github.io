Model tests

I gave the same tasks to several AI models. A script checked the text answers against an answer key; an AI assistant gave the image and video scores, and I then checked those scores myself. What I did not measure is marked as such.

I asked each model 10 questions from the MIHENK open set three times (the Claude models once) and report the median accuracy.

The three Gemini models scored 10/10 in every run and gpt-oss-120b-medium had a median of 9/10, while qwen3-8b, the best local model, had a median of 7/10 (best run 8/10). With only 10 questions and L2 as the hardest level in the MIHENK open set, the top models hit the ceiling and could not be told apart.

Format counts the runs whose raw answer was valid JSON with exactly the keys 1 to 10, without any cleanup; the 10 questions are L2 questions whose answers are not numbers, and L2 is the hardest level in the public MIHENK set (L3 and L4 are only in the hidden set).

Time is wall-clock time of the whole call, including model loading for local runs; the Claude models ran as Claude Code subagents with their own system prompt.

Model names are given as named in the Google Antigravity CLI (agy), which ran the Gemini and gpt-oss models on a subscription.

Image and video models, same prompt

One prompt went to three local image models, two local video models and one cloud video model (Gemini Omni). The local models used the same seed (42), 16:9 and no negative prompt. I kept one output per model and did not cherry-pick (Zeroscope was rerun with fewer frames and steps after its first try took over 15 minutes, and AnimateDiff needed five runs).

A wide shot of a small street market stall at dusk. Exactly three red apples and two yellow lemons sit on a wooden table. A wooden sign above the stall reads "OPEN" in white capital letters. A smiling woman in a blue apron stands behind the table and waves her right hand. Warm lantern light, shallow depth of field.

The local video tests ran on a 16 GB Mac. AnimateDiff is trained for 16 frames but ran with 8. It finished on the fifth run, which split generation and frame decoding into two processes: the memory watchdog stopped three earlier runs (two on the GPU, one on the CPU while decoding frames) because swap use got too high, one CPU run hit the time limit, and the output is noise. FLUX was never tried.

Best image: SDXL-Turbo (12/20); worst: SDXL base (9/20). No local model wrote "OPEN" correctly, and the best video is Gemini Omni (21/25), a cloud model, ahead of Zeroscope (10/25) and AnimateDiff (7/25). This is one prompt and, for the local models, one seed, so it is not a general ranking.

Gemini Omni ran in the Gemini app (Pro subscription) with one try and no seed setting: the sign and the wave are right, the counts are wrong (4 apples and 3 lemons), and cost is 3 because it needs a paid subscription.
