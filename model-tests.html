Model tests

I gave the same tasks to several AI models. A script checked the text answers against an answer key; the image and video scores are a first assessment that I have not checked by eye yet. What I did not measure is marked as such.

Prompt benchmark: 10 questions, 10 models

I asked each model 10 questions from the MIHENK open set three times (the Claude models once) and report the median accuracy.

The three Gemini models scored 10/10 in every run and gpt-oss-120b-medium had a median of 9/10, while the best local model, qwen3-8b, reached 7/10. With only 10 questions and L2 as the hardest level in the MIHENK open set, the top models hit the ceiling and could not be told apart.

Format counts the runs whose raw answer was valid JSON with exactly the keys 1 to 10, without any cleanup; L2 is the harder of the two difficulty levels (L1 and L2) in the MIHENK open set.

Time is wall-clock time of the whole call, including model loading for local runs; the Claude models ran as Claude Code subagents.

Image and video models, same prompt

One prompt went to three local image models, two local video models and one cloud video model (Gemini Omni). The local models used the same seed (42), 16:9 and no negative prompt, with one try each and no cherry-picking.

A wide shot of a small street market stall at dusk. Exactly three red apples and two yellow lemons sit on a wooden table. A wooden sign above the stall reads "OPEN" in white capital letters. A smiling woman in a blue apron stands behind the table and waves her right hand. Warm lantern light, shallow depth of field.

The local video tests ran on a 16 GB Mac. AnimateDiff is trained for 16 frames but ran with 8; it finished on the fifth try after the GPU ran out of memory twice, one CPU run hit the time limit and the memory watchdog stopped another, and its output is noise. FLUX was never tried.

Best image: SDXL-Turbo (12/20); worst: SDXL base (9/20). No local model wrote "OPEN" correctly, and the best video is Gemini Omni (21/25), a cloud model, ahead of Zeroscope (10/25) and AnimateDiff (7/25). This is one prompt with one seed, so it is not a general ranking.
